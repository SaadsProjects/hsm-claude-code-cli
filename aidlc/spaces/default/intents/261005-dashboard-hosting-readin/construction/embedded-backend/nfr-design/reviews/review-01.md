### Summary

The design traces every NFR to a concrete mechanism and its R2 correction holds against the code. Nothing Critical was found and there is one Major, so the verdict is READY. The Major is that the replacement signal and the purpose of the cache clear are unspecified. The weak spots are a probe that cannot detect a wedged server loop and a runtime-failed audit trail that never triggers replacement.
