### Summary

The requirements are well structured, trace to the Q&A and affirmed rules, and the contradictions raised in Q11 and Q12 are resolved explicitly. NOT-READY rests on four Major gaps. The anonymous-visitor check in FR8.1 is not observable over plain HTTP. The staging-to-production gate cannot prove which build was checked. FR5.1 contradicts the open local-sign-in question. The production-branch protection in FR2.2 is not shown to be feasible on a personal repo. A fifth Major finding (R-05) says the departure from the affirmed private-app practice is not recorded. Each can be closed with a short edit before design starts.
