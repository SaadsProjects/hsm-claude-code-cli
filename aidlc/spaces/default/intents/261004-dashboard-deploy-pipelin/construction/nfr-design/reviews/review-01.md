### Summary

Four Major findings (R-01 to R-04) exceed the allowed two. The main concern is that the promotion workflow, the safety-critical piece of the design, cannot work as described: Environment approval cannot follow steps inside the same job, and the permissions listed cannot read check runs. The anonymous-access check that provides the NFR1.1 evidence is also under-specified for Streamlit Cloud's iframe, and the 745-test baseline has no enforcing design. The sign-in gate, secret handling, loopback binding and build-identifier designs are otherwise sound and implementable.
