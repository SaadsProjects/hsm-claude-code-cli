### Summary

The security requirements are testable and traceable, but four Major gaps remain: the local propagation of the new fail-closed signing secret (R-01), a no-click rule that contradicts waking a hibernated app (R-02), an SHA mechanism that may not work on Streamlit Cloud (R-03), and the missing branch restriction on the production Environment (R-04). Together they are more than two Majors, so the verdict is NOT-READY.
